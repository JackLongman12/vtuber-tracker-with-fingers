Train your face

About 2 minutes

Guided expressions teach it your smiles, winks, brows, tongue and cheeks. Follow the prompts over your camera on the Live page; Cancel stops it. Tracking pauses meanwhile.

Smoothing tuned to you

Record a take, then tune

Record about a minute of yourself: talk, turn your head, wave, cross your hands, make fists, then sit still for 10 s. Tuning learns how much to smooth from it.

Custom gestures

Your own hand signs show expressions

Record each sign for 8 seconds on the Live page, and once “none”: relaxed hands that aren't a sign. Then train. Each sign can show an expression while you hold it.

Connect your VTuber app

Turn on VMC protocol receiving on port 39539 (VSeeFace: General settings › OSC/VMC receiver). In OBS, add a browser source with http://127.0.0.1:8000.
